Happy birthday, my love. Today isn’t just another day on the calendar—it’s the day the world became softer, brighter, and infinitely more interesting, because it got you. I’ve been thinking about how to put a whole year of feelings into words, and I keep realizing that language is too small for what you are to me. But I’ll try anyway, because you deserve at least an attempt.

There are people who walk into your life and rearrange the furniture, and then there are people who walk in and somehow make the whole house feel like home. You did the second one. Quietly. Without asking for credit. You made the ordinary parts of my life—the coffee runs, the late-night conversations, the nothing-moments—feel like the best parts. I don’t know how you do it, but I hope you never stop.

I love the way you laugh at your own jokes before you finish them. I love the way you care about things so deeply, even when you pretend you don’t. I love that you can be stubborn and gentle in the same breath, and that you make me want to be a better person without ever asking me to change. You are the most genuine person I know, and every day I get to know you a little more, I feel like I’ve won something I didn’t even know I was playing for.

On your birthday, I want you to know that you are allowed to take up space. You are allowed to want things, to rest, to be celebrated. You spend so much of your time making the world easier for everyone around you—today is the day the world (and me, especially) gets to make it easier for you. Eat the cake. Wear the outfit you’ve been saving. Sleep in. Be a little dramatic about how good your day is going. You’ve earned every bit of it.

I don’t know what the next year will bring, but I know this: I want to be there for it. For the big moments and the tiny ones. For the celebrations and the quiet Tuesday nights. I want to keep learning you, keep laughing with you, keep choosing you. You are my favorite person, my safest place, and my best adventure all at once.

Happy birthday, beautiful. Thank you for being born. Thank you for being you. Thank you for letting me love you. I hope this year gives you everything you’ve been quietly hoping for, and I hope I get to watch you receive it all.

— and I'm sorry. For real. 🥺❤️
